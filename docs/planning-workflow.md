# Planning workflow

Capture → organize → plan → work → review. Status and dates remain separate. List, Board and Timeline present the same records.

Plan My Day has four steps: select available work (including older unfinished, Someday and routines); order/shape work and choose up to three focus tasks; review capacity/dependencies/deadlines/weather/travel; save. Interrupted choices are preserved. A plan is person/day-specific and scheduling feedback is advisory.

Today shows only intentionally planned work plus a separate previous-three-days unfinished section. Older work remains in All Tasks/planning. No automatic rollover or replanning occurs.

Calendar recurrence creates fresh occurrences and retains done/missed/skipped outcomes. Completion-relative recurrence schedules the next occurrence from actual completion. Routine application creates independent tasks from ordered templates. Copying also creates independent tasks and resets progress; moving retains identity and history. Both bulk scheduling previews clear time slots.

Time spent, focus countdown and deadline countdown are separate. One timer per person survives reopening. Reminders operate while open and surface missed alerts on return.

End-of-day review records reflection and explicit choices for unfinished work. Neutral wording, history and recoverability take priority over streaks or scores.
