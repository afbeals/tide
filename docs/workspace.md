# Shared workspace and people

There are no accounts, sessions, PINs or permissions. Everyone with access can inspect and edit everything. Selecting Alex or Sam changes planning context; task ownership is separate shared data.

Each person has a stable ID, optional retired flag, availability, person/day plans, up to three focus selections, daily note, essentials and one active focus timer. Different people can have timers running simultaneously. All people shows their independent workload and context.

Tasks may be Unassigned. Adding unassigned work to a person's plan requires an explicit ownership choice. Reassignment retains content/status/dates, removes prior plan/focus membership, offers explicit planning for the new owner, and never transfers a timer. Retirement keeps ownership and history rather than deleting the person.
